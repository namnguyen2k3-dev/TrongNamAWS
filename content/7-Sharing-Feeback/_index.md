After participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I had the opportunity to gain new knowledge about Cloud, AWS, and how to deploy solutions in real-world environments. Below are some comments and suggestions that may contribute to further improving the program.

### Overall Evaluation of the Program

**1. Training Content and Learning Roadmap**

The program content is structured from fundamental knowledge to practical implementation, helping participants gradually become familiar with AWS services. The roadmap covers Cloud Computing basics, core services (IAM, S3, EC2, DynamoDB, Lambda), and advanced topics like Generative AI (Amazon Bedrock). The labs and accompanying materials provide opportunities to actively explore and verify knowledge through hands-on practice.

**2. Workshops and Knowledge-Sharing Activities**

The workshops provided many practical perspectives from experienced professionals in the industry. The sharing sessions about how Cloud and AI technologies are used in businesses helped provide a clearer understanding of the differences between theoretical knowledge and practical requirements when deploying systems.

**3. Opportunities for Hands-on AWS Practice**

One of the highlights of the program is the opportunity to directly practice with AWS services. Through building a Serverless AI Backend system with **Lambda, API Gateway, Bedrock, DynamoDB, and S3**, I gained a better understanding of the relationships between components in a real-world Cloud architecture.

**4. Developing Problem-Solving Skills**

During the hands-on practice, many configuration errors occurred (Lambda Timeout, IAM Permission, CORS), requiring me to proactively read CloudWatch Logs, check configurations, and identify the causes before determining appropriate solutions. This helped improve my ability to analyze and solve problems rather than simply following predefined instructions.

**5. Connecting with the Technology Community**

The program provided an environment where interns could exchange knowledge, share experiences, and support one another throughout the learning process. Events like the AWS Community Meetup helped expand professional connections and stay updated with the latest technology trends.

---

### Survey Questions

- **What impressed you most about the program?**

  The opportunity to practice directly on real AWS services and build a complete system from scratch. The approach combining Cloud theory, practical workshops, and individual projects helped reinforce knowledge much more deeply compared to learning theory alone.

- **What could the program improve?**

  The program could add more scenario-based labs, where learners are given a general requirement and asked to analyze it, select appropriate AWS services, design the architecture, and deploy the system before comparing their approach with the provided solution.

- **Would you recommend the program to other students?**

  Yes. The program is suitable for students who want to learn about Cloud and AWS through hands-on practice, especially those interested in Backend Development, DevOps, or integrating AI into software projects.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Suggestion:** Add more **Mini Project**-based group assignments, focusing on requirements analysis, architecture design, deployment, and monitoring of a complete system on AWS. Additionally, a module on **AWS GameTech** (Amazon GameLift, latency optimization for games) would be very useful for game development students.

- **Expectation:** Continue organizing workshops and practical experience-sharing sessions, while expanding topics related to **Cloud Security, Advanced Serverless, AI/ML on AWS, and DevOps Automation**.
